FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are investment returns guaranteed?

No. Investment returns should never be represented as guaranteed unless the product and legal framework genuinely support that claim.

Can the website accept Bitcoin?

A real BTC payment system requires a controlled wallet/custodian, blockchain transaction verification and appropriate compliance controls.

Can users withdraw?

A production withdrawal system must connect to a properly configured bank, payment processor or crypto custody service.
